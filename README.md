## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I am **Tsuki Kami**, a German Software Developer with a strong passion for backend systems, clean architecture, and logic-based programming.  
Fluent in **German** and **English**, I’m also on a journey of learning **Japanese (日本語)**.  

In **2025**, I successfully completed my apprenticeship as *Fachinformatiker für Anwendungsentwicklung* at **NAVAYA GmbH** and started my first full-time role at **S-Markt & Mehrwert GmbH & Co. KG**. My long-term goal is to grow into a **Software Architect**.  

My experience spans across **.NET development** (Azure Functions, Razor & Blazor, MVC, Web APIs, Console & WinForms apps, libraries), and I have expanded my stack with **Node.js (Fastify)**, **Kubernetes**, and even **Kotlin** for Minecraft plugins and mods (1.21.x).  

Beyond work, I love diving into new programming languages, keeping up with the latest tech trends, and developing creative projects such as **Minecraft mods, plugins, Lua ComputerCraft scripts, and bots for Discord & Microsoft Teams**.
